Use the recipient’s two shielded public keys. A recipient can connect first, fill their keys and deploy, then share the public contract details with a donor.

What is shared, and what is proved?

The verifier receives the demo taxpayer label, amount, recipient, time window and private receipt opening. It checks that the values match the receipt commitment and that the selected indexer reports it in the contract’s state.

A successful check is not a government-issued tax receipt or confirmation of deductibility. The user-provided label is not a verified legal identity. The configured indexer is a trusted data source, not an independently checked chain-inclusion proof.

Your private file is unencrypted. Anyone with it can read the disclosure. The recipient’s public view does not receive the label or private opening through this app. Timing, token flows and other metadata may still reveal information.
